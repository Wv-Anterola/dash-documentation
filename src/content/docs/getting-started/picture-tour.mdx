title: Dash picture tour
description: Learn the whole shape of Dash with pictures and deliberately simple explanations before opening the detailed guides.
---

You do not need to understand databases, research software, or code to use
this page. Start with one sentence:

> **Dash is a very large board where you place things, connect them, and choose
> different ways to look at them.**

This tour names only nine ideas. Every advanced feature in Dash is made from
some combination of them.

## 1. A workspace is one saved board

![The Dash home screen showing a saved workspace named Dashboard 1.](/assets/images/current/dash-home-workspaces.webp)

The home screen is a shelf of saved workspaces. Click one to open it.

- **Workspace** means the whole saved board.
- **Dashboard** is an older name still used by parts of the interface.
- Opening a workspace does not make a copy. You are opening the saved thing.

## 2. The grey area is the canvas

![A Dash workspace with notes, a map, a planner, and other documents placed on the canvas.](/assets/images/current/dash-trip-planner-workspace.webp)

The canvas is the large area in the middle. You can pan across it and zoom in
or out. Think of it as a desk that is much larger than the screen.

- Drag the empty background to move your view.
- Use the **–** control near the lower-left corner to zoom out.
- Drag a document to move the document.
- Drag a document corner to resize it.

## 3. Every thing on the canvas is a document

“Document” is Dash's name for a saved object. A document can be a note, image,
PDF, map, video, task, chart, collection, agent, or something a project added.

![A collection of different Dash document types and interface components.](/assets/images/visuals/document-model.webp)

This rule is why different things can share the same basic actions:

- move it;
- resize it;
- put it inside a collection;
- link it to something else;
- show it in another place;
- give it fields, tags, and permissions.

The word **node** usually means a document as it appears in a view or graph. It
does not mean a separate kind of saved object.

## 4. Tools make new documents

![The Tools panel showing creator buttons beside the current Dash workspace.](/assets/images/current/dash-tools-panel.webp)

Click **Tools** on the left. Each small labeled icon is a creator.

- Clicking a creator makes its document in Dash's default place.
- Dragging a creator onto the canvas lets you choose the position.
- **Note** makes writing.
- **Image** makes a picture document.
- **Col** means collection: a box that can hold other documents.
- **Map** makes a map.
- **Task** makes a checkable task.
- **Agent** makes an AI helper that can use allowed tools.

Some creator labels make a whole prepared workspace rather than one primitive
document. **Trip Planner** and **API Demo** are examples. The
[complete creator dictionary](/reference/interface-controls/#tools-palette-every-current-creator)
explains every label one at a time.

## 5. The left buttons open drawers

![The Search drawer open on the left side of a Dash workspace.](/assets/images/current/dash-search-panel.webp)

The narrow left strip contains drawers:

- **Search** finds documents.
- **Files** lists saved and recently closed documents.
- **Tools** creates documents.
- **Imports** brings outside files into Dash.
- **Shared** shows items other people shared with you.
- **Trails** manages presentation paths.

Clicking the same drawer button again closes it.

## 6. Properties are the knobs for one selected thing

![The Properties panel open on the right side of a Dash workspace.](/assets/images/current/dash-properties-panel.webp)

Click a document, then open the panel on the right. The panel changes because
an image needs different knobs from a map or a note.

- **Title** changes its displayed name.
- **Type** says what renderer or view is showing it.
- **Fields & Tags** stores named facts about it.
- **Options** changes appearance and interaction.
- **Linked To** lists its relationships.
- **Transform** changes size and position.
- **Other Contexts** shows other places where the same document appears.

Changing a property changes the selected document or its current layout. It
does not usually change every document in Dash.

## 7. A collection is a document that holds documents

![The same collection content shown through several Dash collection views.](/assets/images/visuals/collection-views.webp)

A folder normally only lists files. A Dash collection can show its children as
a freeform canvas, table, stack, board, calendar, map, graph, or another view.
Changing the view does not destroy or convert the children. It changes how the
same children are arranged.

## 8. A link says two things belong together

![Dash documents connected by visible relationship lines.](/assets/images/visuals/linking-annotation.webp)

A link can connect:

- one whole document to another;
- one paragraph to a note;
- one rectangle in an image to another document;
- one time range in a video or recording to an annotation.

Links work in both directions. Following a link finds the other endpoint; it
does not move or copy the destination.

## 9. A trail remembers an order

![The Trails drawer open beside a current Dash workspace.](/assets/images/current/dash-trails-panel.webp)

A trail is a list of documents or saved views in presentation order. It is
similar to slides, except the items can remain live and connected.

- **New trail** starts a path.
- **Pin** adds a document or view.
- Reorder pins to change the story.
- Present the trail to move through it.

## The three clicks to remember

If you forget everything else:

1. Click **Tools** to make something.
2. Click a document to select it.
3. Open **Properties** to understand or change the selected thing.
