## Cohorts

Rosters come from commit authorship in Dash-Web over each date range,
cross-checked against the team list the site carried from 2021. Git is the
stronger source: the earlier team page omitted most of the 2019 and 2020
contributors, which is where more than half of all work on Dash happened.

## Keeping this current

Rosters live in `src/data/dash.ts`, so adding next year's cohort is one edit in
one file. The [documentation guide](/contributing/documentation/) explains the
process.
