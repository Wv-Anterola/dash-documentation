Dash is worked on by students, mostly for a semester or two at a time, through
independent study. That is the unit the project actually moves in, so it is the
unit this history is organised around.

Each cohort page lists the available contributor record and the projects linked
to that period. Project status is based on implementation evidence in the
current application, surviving development branches, and archived research
artifacts. A working branch is recorded as implemented until its behavior is
integrated into the current application.

## What counts as a cohort here

The boundaries are approximate. People overlap terms, some stay for years, and
a few of the strongest contributors are staff rather than students and appear
across every period.

For the two earliest periods we grouped two calendar years together. The record
from before the documentation site existed is thinner, and splitting it further
would imply a precision we do not have.

## The shape of the history

Three phases, roughly.

**2019 to 2020** built the system. The document model, collections, links, and
the view types all date from here, and none has needed fundamental revision.

**2021 to 2023** consolidated. Releases 0.4 through 0.8 shipped, the component
library was split out, this documentation site was created, and Dash started
being used in CS1951V.

**2024 to 2026** is the AI period. Generative features spread across document
types in 2024, the agent tool registry arrived in 2025, and by 2026 most new
work extends the agent rather than sitting beside it.
