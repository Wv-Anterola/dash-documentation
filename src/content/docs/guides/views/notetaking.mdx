---
title: Notetaking view
description: The multi-column stacking view, for reading in one column while writing in another.
---

Several scrollable stacks of documents shown side by side. It is the
multi-column form of [stacking view](/guides/views/stacking/), arranged so you
can read in one column and write in another.

## What it suits

- Taking notes beside the material they are about
- Comparing two or more documents without switching tabs
- Sorting documents into categories by dragging between columns

## Adding documents and columns

**+ New** at the bottom of a stack adds a document to that stack.

**+ New column** at the bottom starts another column.

Documents drag between columns, which is also how you recategorise them when the
columns are grouped by a key.

## Related

If you want the columns to mean something, group them by a field: see
[stacking view](/guides/views/stacking/) for how grouping works.

For notes that live inside another document rather than beside it, see
[annotation and markup](/guides/features/markup/), which covers annotating a
selection and keeping the link back to it.

Notetaking is one value of `CollectionViewType`, like every other view, which is
why switching into it converts nothing. See
[collections and views](/concepts/collections/) for that decision and
[the founding collection work](/research/cohorts/2019-2020/#collections-and-view-types)
for where it came from.
