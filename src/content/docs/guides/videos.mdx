Recorded walkthroughs of the basics. They were made for an earlier version of
the interface, so controls have moved and some have been renamed. The document
model, the view system, and the linking model they describe have not changed,
which is why the recordings are still worth watching.

Where a video and a page disagree about where a control is, the page is
current and the video is not.
