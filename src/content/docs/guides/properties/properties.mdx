title: The properties panel
description: The right-hand panel showing the selected document's contexts, links, display options, and fields, and which sections require developer mode.
---

The properties panel shows the characteristics of whatever is selected: a
document, or the current view if nothing is selected. Its top line gives the
name and the document type; below that are collapsible sections.

## Opening it

Click the left-facing caret on the right edge of the screen, or the double
left-facing carets in the top right, below the heart icon.

## The sections

Four sections are available in novice mode.

| Section | What it shows |
| :------ | :------------ |
| [Other contexts](/guides/properties/othercontexts/) | Other places the selected document also appears |
| [Linked to](/guides/properties/linkedto/) | Documents linked to the selected one |
| [Options](/guides/properties/options/) | Display and behaviour settings for the selection |
| [Fields and tags](/guides/properties/fieldsandtags/) | The document's own key-value fields |

Developer mode adds four more, including
[layout](/guides/properties/layout/),
[filters](/guides/properties/filters/), and
[sharing and permissions](/guides/properties/sharingpermissions/).

## Why fields are editable here

A Dash document is a set of typed key-value fields, and the panel is a direct
view onto them. That is why adding a field here and adding a column in
[schema view](/guides/views/schema/) do the same thing: both write a field onto
the document.

[Documents and fields](/concepts/documents/) explains the model underneath, and
why collections and links are documents with fields too.
