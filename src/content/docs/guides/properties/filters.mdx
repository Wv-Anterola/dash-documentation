---
title: Filters
description: The developer-mode properties section that narrows a view to documents matching chosen field values.
---

:::note[Developer mode]
This section of the properties panel appears only in developer mode.
:::

Filtering narrows what a view shows, based on the documents' own fields. It
hides documents rather than removing them: clearing the filter brings everything
back.

## Creating a filter

Choose a field from the dropdown. The values that field takes across the
collection appear below it as checkboxes.

Ticking a value keeps the documents that carry it. Documents that do not are
hidden until you remove the filter.

## Collapsing and removing

Two controls sit to the right of each field heading.

**Collapse**, the square, hides that field's options. Any filter you already set
stays active, and is shown in bold under the heading so a collapsed filter is
not an invisible one.

**Remove**, the circle, takes the field out of the panel and clears any of its
values you had selected.

## Related

Filters read the same fields you see in
[fields and tags](/guides/properties/fieldsandtags/) and as columns in
[schema view](/guides/views/schema/).

For finding a document rather than narrowing a view, use
[search](/guides/features/search/), which matches metadata across the whole
dashboard.
