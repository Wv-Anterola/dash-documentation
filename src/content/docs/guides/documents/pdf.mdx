PDF files brought in from outside Dash.

## Creating one

Drag a PDF in from your desktop or another application.

![Screen recording: opening and scrolling a PDF document in Dash.](/assets/gifs/environment/pdf.gif)

## Reading and navigating

Scroll through the document, read the current page number, or type a page number
to jump to it.

To search inside the PDF, click the search icon at the bottom right and type a
query. The first match is highlighted in orange and the rest in yellow; the up
and down arrows step through them, and the current match stays orange.

This in-document search is separate from the
[search flyout](/guides/features/search/), which matches document metadata
across the dashboard rather than text inside a file.

## Annotating

- Embed highlights, marquee selections, annotations, and ink on the overlay.
- Click the **Comment** icon to open the annotation margin, which is a place to
  put annotation documents beside the page rather than on top of it. Drag the
  icon to resize the margin.

A highlight or a marquee here is a link anchor, so a link can point at a passage
on page forty rather than at the file. That is the behaviour
[links and anchors](/concepts/links/) exists to explain, and PDFs are where most
people meet it first.

## Summarising a selection

Select text and click **Summarize with AI** in the menu that appears.

The summary is generated into a document, so it can be linked and annotated like
anything else; see [generative media](/concepts/generative/).
