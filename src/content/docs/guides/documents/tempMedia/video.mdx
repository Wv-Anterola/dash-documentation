Video documents play back imported video clips.

## Creating one

Import a video file through the import menu, or drag one in from outside Dash.

## What you can do

- Play, pause, scrub to a point, and toggle fullscreen with the controls at the
  top right.
- Embed annotations and ink over the video by typing or drawing on it, or by
  dragging a document onto it.
- Toggle the timeline with the eye icon at the top right. It works like the
  [audio](/guides/documents/tempmedia/audio/) timeline for placing labels and
  region markers, but video cannot be trimmed.

![Screen recording: scrubbing a video document along its timeline.](/assets/gifs/video/timelineview.gif)

A region on that timeline can anchor a link, so following the link plays that
stretch of video. See [temporal media](/guides/documents/tempmedia/temporal-media/)
for why time is treated as an addressable axis.

## Not the same as generated video

This page is about playing video you already have. Dash also has a separate
document type that plans and generates a video scene by scene, added in 2026;
that one is covered under
[generative AI](/guides/features/generative-ai/).
