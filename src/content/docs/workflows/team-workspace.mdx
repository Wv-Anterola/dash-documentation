## Outcome

A shared corpus whose naming, metadata, links, views, and permissions remain
understandable when contributors change.

## 1. Define the shared vocabulary

Before importing at scale, agree on:

- Collection boundaries
- Required source fields
- Relationship descriptions
- Status values
- Document title format
- Who may edit shared structures

Keep the vocabulary small. A field no one applies consistently is less useful
than a short, well-defined schema.

## 2. Separate source, synthesis, and presentation

Use distinct collections or fields for:

- Original source material
- Individual annotations
- Shared synthesis
- Presentation trails
- Archived or superseded work

The documents can still be linked and embedded across those contexts. The
separation clarifies ownership and review expectations.

## 3. Apply least-necessary access

Give collaborators only the access required for their role. Verify important
nested documents separately: a collection layout may reference a document the
recipient cannot open, or an alias may expose a document in another context.

Never use screenshots as the only permission test. Open the shared workspace as
a lower-privilege account when possible.

## 4. Make review visible

Use fields such as owner, review status, and last-reviewed date. Use links for
substantive relationships, not as a replacement for workflow state.

Suggested review states:

- Unreviewed
- In review
- Verified
- Needs revision
- Superseded

Record disagreement in a linked note instead of overwriting another
contributor's interpretation without context.

## 5. Maintain the workspace

Schedule periodic cleanup:

- Resolve untitled documents.
- Find broken or unexplained links.
- Inspect disconnected graph components.
- Archive abandoned drafts.
- Check group membership and permissions.
- Verify that key trails still open the intended views.
- Export or back up critical sources.

## Definition of done

- Contributors understand the schema.
- Source and synthesis material are distinguishable.
- Permissions were tested.
- Ownership and review state are visible.
- Links have meaningful descriptions.
- The workspace has an explicit maintenance owner.
