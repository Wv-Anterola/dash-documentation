## What these years were about

Over half of every commit ever made to Dash-Web was made in 2019 and 2020. That
is where the system came from.

The decisions from this period are the ones nobody has needed to revisit. A
single document abstraction with typed fields and prototype inheritance, so a
PDF and a collection and a link are all the same kind of thing. Collections that
are themselves documents, with the display style as a property rather than a
separate object. Links that anchor to a region, a selection, or a timestamp
instead of to a file.

The concrete evidence that these held up is that the files where you register a
new document type in 2026 are the same files, with the same functions, that were
written in 2019. The graph view added this year is one more value in the
`CollectionViewType` enum first populated then.

## Caveat on this period

{: .caveat }
> This is the least well-documented period and the hardest to attribute. Several
> prolific 2019 contributors commit only under a first name or a handle and we
> could not resolve them to full names; they are recorded as unresolved in the
> people data rather than dropped. The projects listed here are architectural
> rather than discrete features, because that is genuinely what the work was,
> but it does mean individual credit is coarser than it should be. Corrections
> from anyone who was there would be welcome.
