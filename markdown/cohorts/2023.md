## What this year was about

Two new document types from this year are still in daily use: the calendar
collection view and the Mapbox-backed map document, both built in a single burst
in December 2023.

The physics simulation platform is the other story from 2023, and it is a
different kind. It was a real system with three modes, it was documented on this
site, and it is not in Dash any more. We have kept both the project entry and
the original documentation page, marked as removed. A research group that
deletes the record of work that did not survive ends up unable to explain its
own history, and the students who built it still built it.

This is also the year Dash was used in CS1951V, the hypertext course, which
produced the student use-case videos linked from the About page.
