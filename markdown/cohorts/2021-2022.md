## What these years were about

Commit volume fell sharply after 2020, from roughly 3,900 in that year to 1,300
and then 1,100. The work changed character with it. Rather than building new
subsystems, this period tidied up what already existed and made it possible for
other people to use.

Releases 0.4 through 0.8 came out of these two years, which is more shipping
than any period before or since. The documentation site was created in August
2021 and most of the feature pages still on it were written in the fortnight
that followed. The shared component library was split out so that features
stopped inventing their own buttons.

The data visualisation document type also dates from here, first committed in
June 2022. It is a good example of how long things last in this project: every
data visualisation project in the four cohorts since has been an extension of
that same document type.

## Caveat on this period

{: .caveat }
> Attribution here is thinner than for later cohorts. The documentation site did
> not exist for most of 2021, branch naming was less consistent, and a large
> share of commits are from the research director rather than from students, so
> individual contributions are harder to separate out. Projects listed for these
> years are ones we could tie to specific commits or to a release note.
